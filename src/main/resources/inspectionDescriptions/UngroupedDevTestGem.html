Reports a well-known development or test gem (RSpec, RuboCop, Pry, FactoryBot, Capybara, SimpleCov, WebMock, VCR and similar) declared at the top level of a Gemfile instead of inside a group :development or group :test block.

A top-level gem is installed in every environment, production included: the bundle grows, and a tool such as Pry becomes reachable where it should not be. The check is a line-based scan, so it works without the Ruby plugin.
